## 1. Product objective

Help a self-directed learner practise **evidence-based judgment** on Apple using the already-verified Sprint 1–3 financial history, while keeping every claim in a labelled layer:

| Layer | What it is | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified SEC facts | Annual Apple figures already returned by TenYearExplorer | A forecast or a complete company picture |
| Deterministic calculations / explanations | Existing YoY, CAGR, margins, FCF, cash conversion, net debt, and `apple-trend-copy` sentences | An opinion about Apple’s quality |
| Familiar analogies | Optional teaching metaphors, always labelled | Apple evidence |
| Learner judgments | The learner’s own recorded / deferred / uncertain stance on a stage | A product score or recommendation |
| Bias reflections | The learner’s self-check against a named educational bias prompt | A diagnosis that the learner *is* biased |
| Counter-evidence | Facts or notes the learner records that could weaken their judgment | A hidden “correct answer” |
| Open questions | What the learner still does not know | A todo list generated by an LLM |

The product must remain **educational decision-support**. It must never produce Buy / Sell / Hold, a rating, a score, a valuation, or a statement that the learner is biased.

Sprint 4 succeeds if a learner can start from **Finance → Apple (SEC)**, walk five existing financial stages, attach selected evidence to the existing thesis loop, and leave with a structured Apple Analysis Summary — including if they never reach a firm judgment.

## 4. Circle of Competence behaviour

Circle of Competence (CoC) is **learner-authored**. The product does not score competence and does not tell the learner they are inside or outside Apple.

### 4.1 Capture

The learner selects zero or more chips and may add a short note.

Suggested chips (editable copy, not a completeness claim):

- Consumer electronics as a user
- Software / platforms as a user
- Retail or brand businesses
- Manufacturing or supply chains
- Financial statements generally
- Cash flow statements
- Balance sheets / debt
- Technology investing generally
- Apple specifically
- None of these / I am still defining it

Empty selection is allowed. Treat it as “not yet defined.”

### 4.2 Deterministic interpretation (not a verdict)

From the selected chips + note, a **template function** produces one short restatement, for example:

> You described your current circle as: {chips}. You have not claimed this is complete. Apple’s Sprint 1–3 evidence in this prototype is limited to reported annual financial history. That history does not prove you are competent in Apple, and it does not prove you are not.

If the note is empty and no chips are selected:

> You have not defined a circle of competence yet. You can still review Apple’s reported facts and leave judgments uncertain.

### 4.3 Confirm / Correct / Skip

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm** | Stores the interpretation as accepted wording. Learner may still edit chips later. |
| **Correct** | Returns to capture; previous interpretation is superseded. |
| **Skip** | Stores `coc.status = skipped`. Analysis continues. Summary shows “Circle of Competence not confirmed.” |

The CoC block appears on the Summary and can be revisited. Changing CoC later does **not** erase stage judgments; the Summary notes the CoC was edited after some judgments.

## 5. Bias presentation timing

Bias checks are **educational prompts**, not assessments.

### 5.1 Introduction (once)

After CoC, before Stage 1, show a short primer:

- These checks exist so the learner pauses before treating a story as a fact.
- Completing a check is not an admission of bias.
- Skipping a check is allowed; the Summary will show the check as skipped.
- The product will never say “you are biased.”

### 5.2 Before every material judgment

A **material judgment** is any stage action of **Record**.

- **Record** requires completing (or explicitly skipping) that stage’s pre-judgment bias check first.
- **Defer** and **Uncertain** do not require a bias check, but the stage still offers one.
- The learner cannot reach the Record confirmation control until the bias-check control has a value (`acknowledged`, `noted`, or `skipped`).

### 5.3 After a recorded judgment

Post-judgment review is required before the analysis can be marked complete, but **not** before leaving the stage. The learner may:

- complete post-judgment immediately, or
- continue and finish post-judgment items in the Bias and Counter-Evidence Review.

### 5.4 What the UI must never do

- No “bias score.”
- No red flag on the person.
- No copy such as “this looks like overconfidence.”
- Allowed copy: “A common trap in this situation is overconfidence. Does that possibility matter to your next sentence?”

## 6. Supported bias library

Ship a **closed, curated library**. Copy is educational and situation-specific. Do not expand beyond this set in Sprint 4.

| ID | Name | When it is offered | Prompt shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `confirmation` | Confirmation seeking | Every stage, especially after a Confirm on the deterministic explanation | “Are you mainly looking for facts that fit a story you already like?” |
| `recency` | Recency / latest year | Revenue & growth; profit vs cash; FCF | “Is the most recent year doing more work than the ten-year path?” |
| `anchoring` | Anchoring | Revenue & growth; cash/debt | “Are you stuck on the first number you saw (start year, peak, or latest)?” |
| `narrative` | Narrative fluency | Profitability; CoC if Apple chip selected | “Is a familiar Apple story standing in for the table?” |
| `halo` | Brand / halo | CoC; cash/debt; summary | “Is admiration for the products doing work the financial facts did not do?” |
| `overconfidence` | Overconfidence | Any Record judgment | “How would your wording change if you were less sure?” |
| `outcome-preview` | Outcome temptation | Post-judgment only | “Are you judging the quality of your process by whether Apple ‘feels’ successful?” |

Assignment rule (deterministic):

- Each financial stage has **one primary** bias and **one optional secondary**.
- The pre-judgment screen shows the primary. The learner may open “Another check” to see the secondary.
- The library file is static TypeScript. No remote fetch. No model.

Stage defaults:

| Stage | Primary | Secondary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue and growth | `recency` | `anchoring` |
| Profitability and margins | `narrative` | `confirmation` |
| Profit versus cash generation | `confirmation` | `recency` |
| CapEx and free cash flow | `overconfidence` | `recency` |
| Cash, debt, position | `halo` | `anchoring` |

## 7. Pre-judgment and post-judgment flows

### 7.1 Pre-judgment

1. Learner has seen facts + deterministic explanation.
2. Learner Confirm / Correct / Skip the explanation.
3. If they choose **Record**:
   - Show primary bias check.
   - They pick: **I’ll keep this in mind** / **I added a note** / **Skip this check**.
   - Then judgment notes + confidence.
4. If they choose **Defer** or **Uncertain**:
   - Bias check is optional.
   - Notes optional.
   - Stage is completable.

### 7.2 Judgment values

| Value | Meaning | Minimum required fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `record` | Learner states a view about **this stage’s evidence**, not about buying Apple | Bias check + judgment text (≥ 1 sentence) + confidence 1–10 |
| `defer` | Not judging this stage now | None beyond the choice |
| `uncertain` | Evidence was reviewed; learner will not take a view | Optional note |

Judgment text must be framed as interpretation of **historical Apple financial evidence**, with a standing disclaimer: not a recommendation.

### 7.3 Post-judgment (after Record, and encouraged after Uncertain)

Five prompts, each a short text field:

1. **Counter-evidence** — which displayed fact or calculation could weaken the judgment?
2. **Alternative explanations** — what else could produce the same numbers?
3. **Missing information** — what is outside Sprint 1–3 scope?
4. **What would change my mind** — a concrete future observation, not a price target
5. **Confidence** — 1–10, with helper text that high confidence is a self-report, not a grade

Deferral: post-judgment fields stay empty and appear as open items on the Review screen.

## 8. Guided financial stages

Reuse the existing Apple API and copy builders. **No new metrics. No new calculator pipeline.**

Each stage declares which existing endpoints / response slices it reads. The UI may call the current Next.js proxy (`/api/companies/AAPL/financial-history?...`) exactly as `FinancialPerformance` does today.

| # | Stage | Existing metric IDs / slices | Deterministic copy to reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Revenue and growth | `revenue` + summary YoY/CAGR | `buildAppleSummarySentences`, `buildAppleBeginnerExplanation` |
| 2 | Profitability and margins | `gross-profit`, `operating-income`, `net-income` + `margins` | `buildAppleMarginExplanation` plus per-metric summaries |
| 3 | Profit versus cash generation | `net-income` and `operating-cash-flow` + cash conversion relationship | Existing relationship labels/formulas from the API payload |
| 4 | CapEx and free cash flow | `capital-expenditure`, `free-cash-flow` | API `formula`, CapEx positive-spend warning, FCF margin |
| 5 | Cash, debt and financial position | `cash-and-equivalents`, `total-debt` + net debt / net cash | Existing cash & financial health section copy |

### 8.1 Stage screen composition

1. **Fact block** — 10-year range, start/end, currency, source badge (`SEC EDGAR fixture` vs live), cache status.
2. **Calculation block** — CAGR / YoY / margin / FCF / net debt already computed by the API.
3. **Explanation block** — existing deterministic sentences. Learner Confirm / Correct / Skip.
4. **Analogy block (optional)** — static, stage-specific metaphor. Visible only after a “Show an analogy” disclosure. Label: `Analogy — not Apple evidence`.
5. Bias check and judgment as in §7.

### 8.2 Analogy examples (illustrative; final copy in implementation)

- Revenue path: “A decade of takings at a shop, not next year’s takings.”
- Margins: “How much of each dollar of sales is left after costs — not why customers came.”
- Profit vs cash: “Profit is the report card; operating cash is money that actually arrived.”
- FCF: “Cash left after maintenance-style spending on the shop — still not a valuation.”
- Net debt: “What is owed minus cash in the till — not the worth of the brand.”

If analogy copy cannot stay this tightly scoped, ship the stage **without** an analogy rather than blurring layers.

### 8.3 Correcting an explanation

**Correct** opens a learner text field: “How would you phrase this instead?” The original deterministic explanation remains visible and labelled. The correction is stored as learner wording, never written back to the API or copy helpers.

## 9. State and persistence model

### 9.1 Why versioned local storage

Today `PrototypeApp` is **in-memory React state** and resets on “Restart walkthrough.” Sprint 4 must persist Apple analysis across refresh, while remaining private and offline.

Use `localStorage` only (not cookies, not server, not IndexedDB for v1).

### 9.2 Storage key and version

```
key: aic.appleGuidedAnalysis.v1
schemaVersion: 1
```

On read:

- Missing key → empty analysis.
- Unknown future version → do not migrate blindly; show “Saved analysis is from a newer version” and keep the raw JSON untouched.
- v1 with missing optional fields → fill defaults.

Write after every successful screen completion (debounced ~300ms on text input).

### 9.3 Proposed document shape

```ts
interface AppleGuidedAnalysisV1 {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  analysisId: string;          // uuid created client-side
  createdAt: string;           // ISO
  updatedAt: string;
  company: "AAPL";
  sourceMode: "fixture" | "live" | "unknown";
  circleOfCompetence: {
    chips: string[];
    note: string;
    interpretation: string;
    status: "empty" | "confirmed" | "corrected" | "skipped";
    revisedAfterJudgment: boolean;
  };
  biasIntroCompletedAt: string | null;
  stages: Record<StageId, StageRecord>;
  review: {
    completedAt: string | null;
  };
  summary: {
    generatedAt: string | null;
    selectedPacketIds: string[];
  };
}

type StageId =
  | "revenue-growth"
  | "profitability-margins"
  | "profit-vs-cash"
  | "capex-fcf"
  | "cash-debt-position";

interface StageRecord {
  explanationStatus: "unseen" | "confirmed" | "corrected" | "skipped";
  explanationCorrection: string;
  analogyShown: boolean;
  biasCheck: {
    biasId: string;
    status: "unseen" | "acknowledged" | "noted" | "skipped";
    note: string;
  } | null;
  judgment: {
    status: "none" | "record" | "defer" | "uncertain";
    text: string;
    confidence: number | null;
  };
  post: {
    counterEvidence: string;
    alternativeExplanations: string;
    missingInformation: string;
    mindChanger: string;
    confidence: number | null;
    completed: boolean;
  };
  evidencePacket?: EvidencePacket; // snapshot of facts shown, not a live recompute contract
}
```

`EvidencePacket` stores **display snapshots** (metric labels, FY window, start/end compact values, sourceType, relationship if later attached to thesis). It is not a second calculation engine. Refreshing the API may change live figures; the packet remains what the learner saw, with a retrieved-at timestamp.

### 9.4 Interaction with existing prototype state

- Keep current `PrototypeState` in React memory for Harborline + single thesis observation.
- Apple guided analysis lives beside it in `localStorage`.
- Restart walkthrough:
  - Always reset in-memory Harborline/thesis/decision state (current behaviour).
  - Prompt: **Keep Apple analysis** / **Clear Apple analysis**. Default: Keep.

### 9.5 No server identity

Do not add `userId`, `agencyId`, auth tokens, or analytics events.

## 10. Thesis / evidence integration

Sprint 2–3 already allow **one** Finance observation (`performanceObservation` + `performanceEvidence`) with a learner-chosen relationship (`supports` / `weakens` / `neutral`).

Sprint 4 must let the learner **save selected evidence and interpretations into the product loop** without replacing the Harborline thesis fields.

### 10.1 Packets

Each completed stage (any judgment status except `none`) can produce a packet:

- Stage name
- Fact snapshot
- Deterministic explanation (original)
- Learner correction (if any)
- Judgment status + text
- Bias check status (not a diagnosis)
- Source: fixture or live

### 10.2 Saving into the loop

From Summary (and optionally from a stage footer):

- **Add to thesis** attaches the packet as evidence.
- Learner must still choose relationship: supports / weakens / neutral.
- Analogies are **never** included in the thesis packet.

### 10.3 Compatibility choice (open — see §20)

**Recommended default for implementation:** extend `ThesisDraft` with `analysisPackets: EvidencePacket[]` and keep the existing single `performanceObservation` working as today. Thesis Builder lists packets as read-only cards with relationship editors.

Do not auto-write judgments into Decision Journal stance. Decision remains a separate, later act.

## 11. Final review and summary

### 11.1 Bias and Counter-Evidence Review

A single scrolling screen listing, for each stage:

- Judgment status
- Bias check status (acknowledged / noted / skipped / not required)
- Counter-evidence text or “not yet written”
- Alternative explanation or “not yet written”
- Missing information
- Mind-changer
- Confidence

Incomplete post-judgment items for **Record** judgments are listed first. The learner can jump back to that stage.

The Review must not rank stages or compute a “bias tally.”

### 11.2 Apple Analysis Summary

A structured, printable-in-spirit (scroll, copy text) composition:

1. Disclaimer — educational, not advice
2. Circle of Competence (learner words + interpretation status)
3. Evidence reviewed (five stages, fact snapshots only)
4. Deterministic explanations (original wording)
5. Learner judgments (record / defer / uncertain)
6. Bias checks completed or skipped
7. Counter-evidence and mind-changers
8. Open questions
9. Packets saved to thesis (if any)

Summary generation is **template assembly** from stored state. No LLM.

Marking the analysis complete requires:

- Bias intro seen
- All five stages visited
- Each stage has judgment `record` | `defer` | `uncertain`
- Every `record` has a pre-judgment bias-check value
- Every `record` has post-judgment fields non-empty **or** the learner explicitly confirms “I am leaving post-judgment incomplete”

The last confirmation exists so a learner can finish without pretending the review is done.
